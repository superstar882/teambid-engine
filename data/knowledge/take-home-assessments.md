# Take-homes and online assessments

Group: Interviews · Related vault file: `Job/Interview Prep/Prep Plan.md` · 6 sources, 11 findings

## Findings (strongest sources first)

- Anthropic asks candidates to complete take-home assessments without Claude unless explicitly told AI is allowed. (Anthropic, 2025; high)
  - Takeaway: Read every take-home's AI policy before starting; rules differ by company and even by test.
- Most surveyed engineers had received a take-home and most think it should take 4 hours or less. **85% received one; over 80% say 4 hours or less; plurality prefer 2 hours; ~700 respondents** (interviewing.io, n.d.; high)
  - Takeaway: Treat a take-home well over 4 hours as a yellow flag and ask about scope.
- HackerRank flags plagiarism using code-similarity thresholds that depend on code length. **75% match for 10+ lines; 90% for shorter code** (HackerRank Support, n.d.; high)
  - Takeaway: Copied or AI-generated code can be flagged even when similar by coincidence; write your own.
- The newer version uses constrained puzzle-style tasks and no visualization tools, so candidates build their own debugging tools. (Anthropic Engineering, 2026-01-21; high)
  - Takeaway: Practice building quick debugging and test harnesses under time pressure.
- Anthropic's performance-team take-home explicitly allowed AI and was redesigned repeatedly as Claude models caught up to human applicants. **window cut from 4 hours to 2; Claude Opus 4.5 matched the strongest candidates** (Anthropic Engineering, 2026-01-21; high)
  - Takeaway: AI-allowed take-homes are getting harder and reward insight over code volume.
- The main complaint is no feedback after submission; candidates also often spend longer than the company estimate. (interviewing.io, n.d.; high)
  - Takeaway: Ask up front whether the take-home will be discussed in a later round.
- Most candidates think take-homes should be paid but almost none are. **58% believe they deserve payment; 4% were paid** (interviewing.io, n.d.; high)
- Copy-paste frequency and tab switching are tracked and visible to reviewers, with code playback available. **suspicious attempts often show "out of window duration greater than 65 seconds"** (HackerRank Support, n.d.; high)
  - Takeaway: Stay in the test window and avoid pasting large blocks during OAs.
- Coding questions are scored by test cases, with the question score the sum of passed test case scores. (HackerRank Support, n.d.; medium)
  - Takeaway: Partial solutions that pass some tests still earn points; submit working code early.
- Many developers admit using AI on coding assessments and most think AI makes gaming them easier. **56% used AI on assessments (per search summary); 76% say AI makes gaming easier; 73% say losing to AI-assisted candidates is unfair** (HackerRank, 2025; medium)
  - Takeaway: Assume assessments are monitored; follow each test's AI rules exactly.
- Ghosting happens even after a take-home assignment or test. **23% ghosted after a take-home or test (per search summary of the report)** (Greenhouse, 2024-12-10; low)
  - Takeaway: Limit unpaid take-home time since a response isn't guaranteed.
