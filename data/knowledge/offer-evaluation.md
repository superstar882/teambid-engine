# Evaluating offers

Group: Offers · Related vault file: `Job/Playbook/Negotiation.md` · 6 sources, 8 findings

## Findings (strongest sources first)

- The Labor Commissioner can impose penalties of $100 to $10,000 per violation of the pay scale rules. **$100 to $10,000 per violation** (California Legislative Information, n.d.; medium)
- The guide also recommends asking about funding history, burn rate, revenue, and acceleration of vesting on acquisition. (Holloway, 2022-09-12; high)
  - Takeaway: For a self-funded company, runway and revenue matter more than headline equity.
- Median total compensation for entry-level software engineers in the report was $155K, up from $152K in 2024. **$155K entry level; $226K software engineer; $312K senior (245K+ data points)** (Levels.fyi, 2025; high)
  - Takeaway: Benchmark for the cash-plus-liquid-stock bar a startup's cash plus risk-adjusted equity is competing against; skewed to large tech companies.
- The guide calls exploding offers a bad practice and says candidates with in-demand skills can ask for more time. (Holloway, 2022-09-12; high)
  - Takeaway: Pressure to sign fast through an agency is a reason to slow down, not speed up.
- A company that won't tell you the total share count or your percentage is offering a very poor deal. **Never accept stock without "the exact number of total shares (or percentage of the company)"** (Holloway, 2022-09-12; high)
  - Takeaway: If the startup or the agency won't disclose percentage, treat that as a red flag.
- The report notes that as startup teams shrink, median compensation for remaining employees has begun to grow. (Carta, 2025; medium)
- The report describes AI/ML as having moved from a niche specialty to one of the largest and highest-paid software engineering tracks in 2025. (Levels.fyi, 2025; medium)
- Median initial equity grants for AI/ML engineers on Carta rose 31% between January 2024 and February 2026, and their median salary rose 9.1%. **+31% equity grant; +9.1% salary** (Carta, 2025; medium)
