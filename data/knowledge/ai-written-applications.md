# AI-written applications

Group: Resume · Related vault file: `Job/Resume/rules/06-ai-tells-in-resumes.md` · 7 sources, 17 findings

## Findings (strongest sources first)

- Researchers found no evidence employers were less satisfied with workers whose resumes were AI-assisted. (MIT Sloan School of Management, 2023-04-26; high)
- In a field experiment with 480,948 jobseekers, those given algorithmic writing assistance on resumes were hired 8% more often and got 7.8% more offers. **+8% hires; +7.8% offers; n=480,948** (MIT Sloan School of Management, 2023-04-26; high)
  - Takeaway: AI for clarity and grammar helps; it is not the same as AI-generated content.
- Galdin and Silbert's Freelancer.com study found employers paid more for customized proposals before LLMs, but that willingness to pay fell sharply after AI writing tools arrived. (Tuck School of Business at Dartmouth, 2026-01-05; high)
  - Takeaway: A polished tailored letter no longer stands out by itself; verifiable proof must carry it.
- On a large online labor platform, an AI cover-letter tool raised textual alignment with job posts and callback rates. (arXiv, 2025-09-29; high)
  - Takeaway: AI help can raise callbacks when used to match the post.
- Greenhouse surveyed 4,136 job seekers and hiring staff in the US, UK, Ireland and Germany; 74% of US job seekers use AI in their search. **74%; n=4,136** (Greenhouse, 2025-11; high)
  - Takeaway: AI use is normal; the differentiator is specificity and proof.
- Time spent editing AI-generated drafts was positively correlated with hiring success. (arXiv, 2025-09-29; high)
- 41% of surveyed candidates admitted using prompt injection (hidden text) to get past screening filters. **41%** (Greenhouse, 2025-11; medium)
  - Takeaway: Never use hidden text; recruiters are alert to it and it reads as fraud.
- 39% of hiring managers now run more in-person interviews to verify candidates. **39%** (Greenhouse, 2025-11; medium)
  - Takeaway: Be ready to defend every resume claim live.
- Their model projects that in a fully AI-saturated market, top-quintile workers are hired 19% less often and bottom-quintile workers 14% more often. **-19% top quintile, +14% bottom quintile** (Tuck School of Business at Dartmouth, 2026-01-05; medium)
  - Takeaway: Strong candidates must signal ability through hard-to-fake evidence (live demos, real metrics).
- Employers on the platform were receiving around 60 applications within minutes and shifted toward price and ratings. **~60 applications within a few minutes** (Tuck School of Business at Dartmouth, 2026-01-05; medium)
  - Takeaway: On Upwork, early bids and reviews matter more as letters lose signal.
- In a Pollfish survey of 600 US hiring managers (May 2025), 33.5% said they can spot an AI-written resume in under 20 seconds. **33.5%; n=600** (TopResume, 2025-05; medium)
  - Takeaway: Generic AI phrasing gets noticed quickly.
- 30.3% said using AI to write the resume is unacceptable, while just over half accept AI for proofreading or drafting help. **30.3%** (TopResume, 2025-05; medium)
  - Takeaway: Use AI for editing, not for inventing content.
- 19.6% would reject a candidate with an AI-generated resume or cover letter. **19.6%** (TopResume, 2025-05; medium)
  - Takeaway: A minority rejects outright, so keep AI output edited and specific.
- 90% reported a spike in low-effort or spammy applications since AI tools spread. **90%** (Resume Now, 2025-03; low)
- In a survey of 925 US HR professionals (March 28, 2025), 62% said AI-generated resumes without customization are more likely to be rejected. **62%; n=925** (Resume Now, 2025-03; low)
  - Takeaway: The penalty is for generic AI text, not AI use itself.
- Resume.io reports that in a survey of 3,000 hiring managers, 49% dismiss resumes they think are AI-generated. **49%; n=3,000** (Resume.io, n.d.; low)
  - Takeaway: Sounding human matters as much as content.
- The report says many hiring managers prefer a less polished authentic resume over a perfectly polished AI one. (Resume.io, n.d.; low)
  - Takeaway: Plain, specific wording beats over-polished prose.
