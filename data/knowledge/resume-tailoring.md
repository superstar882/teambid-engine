# Tailoring to a job post

Group: Resume · Related vault file: `Job/Resume/rules/09-tailoring-playbook.md` · 8 sources, 9 findings

## Findings (strongest sources first)

- AI-mentioning postings were 134% above the Feb 2020 baseline while total postings were only 6% above it. **+134% vs +6%** (Indeed Hiring Lab, 2026-01-22; high)
  - Takeaway: AI-titled roles are where demand is growing in a weak market.
- About 45% of US data & analytics postings and 20%+ of software development postings mentioned AI in December 2025. **45% data & analytics; 20%+ software development** (Indeed Hiring Lab, 2026-01-22; high)
- MIT advises analyzing the job description for recurring terms and emphasizing requirements listed first in the posting. (MIT Career Advising & Professional Development (CAPD), 2025; high)
  - Takeaway: Put the JD's top-listed skill (e.g., RAG, PySpark) in the first bullets and summary.
- After the tool launched, the correlation between cover-letter/job-post alignment and callbacks fell by 51%, and employers shifted toward prior work history. **51% drop** (arXiv, 2025-09-29; high)
  - Takeaway: Keyword alignment alone is losing value; concrete past work and proof links matter more.
- 76.4% of recruiters surveyed by Jobscan said they search and rank candidates by skills from the job description. **76.4%** (Jobscan, 2026-08-20; medium)
  - Takeaway: Use the JD's exact skill names (e.g., 'LangChain', 'PySpark') so recruiter searches hit.
- 44% of surveyed job seekers got zero interviews in the previous month, showing a tough market. **44%** (Jobscan, 2025-02-25; medium)
- Jobscan's analysis of 2.5M+ applications found that matching the job title on the resume was associated with a 10.6x higher interview rate. **10.6x** (Jobscan, 2025-02-25; low)
  - Takeaway: Use the posting's exact title (e.g., 'AI Engineer') in the resume headline when honest.
- 78% said personalized details signal genuine interest and fit. **78%** (Resume Now, 2025-03; low)
  - Takeaway: Reference the company's actual product or stack.
- Recruiters cited clear skimmable structure, relevant experience and job-specific tailoring without keyword stuffing as what works. **Tailoring without stuffing 76%** (IT Brief UK, 2025-11-19; low)
  - Takeaway: Tailor, but don't paste keyword lists.
