# Data engineer job market

Group: Market · Related vault file: `Job/Resume/rules/10-keyword-bank.md` · 5 sources, 8 findings

## Findings (strongest sources first)

- Database architects earn a median wage well above $100K. **$135,980 median (May 2024); DBAs $104,620** (U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, 2025; high)
- BLS has no separate data engineer code; the closest proxy, database administrators and architects, is projected to grow about as fast as average. **4% growth 2024-2034; about 7,800 openings per year** (U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, 2025; high)
  - Takeaway: Official data shows steady, not explosive, data-engineering demand.
- Data and analytics is the occupation group where AI terms show up most often in postings. **Nearly 45% of data & analytics postings include AI-related terms** (Indeed Hiring Lab, 2026-01-22; high)
- Skills in AI, data, and cloud engineering all gained value between 2023 and 2024. **"accelerating value between 2023 and 2024"** (Dice, 2025-01-10; medium)
- Very few data engineer postings explicitly target entry level, and most do not state years required. **2.3% up to 2 years; ~70% not specified** (365 Data Science, 2026-04-23; medium)
  - Takeaway: Don't self-filter out of postings without stated years.
- Average data engineer salary cited from Glassdoor is about $153K, and remote postings are a small minority. **$153,000 average; 10.1% remote** (365 Data Science, 2026-04-23; medium)
- Entry-level data engineer postings are rare and senior+ roles dominate. **3% entry-level (219 of 6,877); 45% senior/staff; median US base $128,300** (DEV Community (InterviewStack.io data), 2026-05-09; medium)
- Spark and distributed-systems skills carry the largest salary premiums in the sample. **Spark ~$19.8K premium; distributed systems/data lake/S3 ~$22K** (DEV Community (InterviewStack.io data), 2026-05-09; low)
