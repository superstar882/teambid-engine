# AI engineer job market

Group: Market · Related vault file: `Job/Resume/rules/05-ai-engineer-resume.md` · 10 sources, 11 findings

## Findings (strongest sources first)

- Indeed's AI tracker (share of postings mentioning AI) hit a record high in December 2025. **4.2% of US postings in Dec 2025** (Indeed Hiring Lab, 2026-01-22; high)
  - Takeaway: AI-labeled roles are the growing slice of a flat market, so lead with LLM/RAG work.
- AI tool use is near universal, but trust in AI accuracy fell, with 'almost right' AI output the top frustration. **84% use or plan to use AI tools; trust in accuracy fell to 29%; 45% cite 'almost right' solutions** (Stack Overflow, 2025-07; high)
- AI skills now appear in a growing share of all US postings, and demand is shifting toward deployment skills like AWS, scalability, and workflow management. **2.5% of all US postings mention AI skills, up 55% from the prior year** (Lightcast, 2026-04-13; high)
  - Takeaway: Stress production deployment (always-on FastAPI server, S3 caching) over demos.
- Top locations for AI engineer jobs are San Francisco, New York City, and Dallas. (Dice, 2026-01-14; high)
- AI engineers are heavily concentrated in two metros. **Over 65% of AI engineers are in San Francisco and New York City** (SignalFire, 2025-05-20; high)
  - Takeaway: Being in the Bay Area is an advantage for onsite/hybrid AI roles.
- Tech professionals who design, develop, or implement AI solutions earn more than peers not doing AI work. **17.7% higher salaries** (Dice, 2025-01-10; high)
  - Takeaway: AI work carries a measurable pay premium; price that in.
- AI engineer ranked #1 on LinkedIn's Jobs on the Rise 2026 list for the US. **#1 fastest-growing role** (Dice, 2026-01-14; high)
  - Takeaway: 'AI Engineer' is the right headline title.
- Demand for AI engineers increased massively, with the Bay Area as the center of gravity, and AI engineering is the exception to declining remote listings. (The Pragmatic Engineer, 2025-09; medium)
  - Takeaway: Bay Area location plus AI engineering focus is the strongest combo in this market.
- Levels.fyi lists a US median total compensation for AI Engineer in the mid-$150Ks (seen via search excerpt; figure updates continuously). **$153,750 median total comp (US)** (Levels.fyi, 2026; medium)
  - Takeaway: Useful anchor for salary talks; early-career Bay Area offers will vary by company tier.
- Search excerpts citing Levels.fyi report an AI-focus pay premium over non-AI engineers that grows with seniority. **14.2% at senior level, 18.7% at staff level** (Levels.fyi, 2026; low)
- Median salary in USD-listed agentic engineering postings is about $213K, with LangChain roles skewing more junior/mid-level. **$213,000 median all agentic roles; 37.8% of LangChain roles junior/mid** (agentic-engineering-jobs.com, 2026-05; low)
