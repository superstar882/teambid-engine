# Follow-up and rejection

Group: Interviews · Related vault file: `Job/Interview Prep/Prep Plan.md` · 5 sources, 10 findings

## Findings (strongest sources first)

- Amazon describes thank-you notes as optional and says feedback typically comes within 2-5 business days. **2-5 business days** (About Amazon, n.d.; high)
  - Takeaway: Don't chase Amazon recruiters before about a week has passed.
- Google reports rejected candidates who went through structured interviews were happier with the experience than those in unstructured ones. **35% happier** (Google re:Work, n.d.; medium)
- Many employers can't give feedback because of legal or time constraints. (Binghamton University Fleishman Center for Career and Professional Development, n.d.; high)
  - Takeaway: Don't read a no-feedback reply as a bad sign; keep the door open for future roles.
- After a rejection, reply promptly, thank them, and politely ask if they're willing to share feedback. (Binghamton University Fleishman Center for Career and Professional Development, n.d.; high)
  - Takeaway: Send a short, gracious feedback request within days of a rejection.
- Send an individual, personalized thank-you email to each interviewer within 24 hours. **within 24 hours** (Northeastern University Graduate Programs, 2017-08-25; high)
  - Takeaway: Reference something specific from each conversation; keep it short.
- Ask for the decision timeline during the interview and follow up only once it has passed. (Northeastern University Graduate Programs, 2017-08-25; high)
  - Takeaway: Always ask 'what are next steps and timing?' at the end of each round.
- Greenhouse data classifies a meaningful share of posted jobs as ghost jobs. **18-22% of jobs each quarter** (Greenhouse, 2024-12-10; medium)
  - Takeaway: Prioritize roles with a real recruiter contact or recent posting.
- A majority of job seekers have been ghosted after a job interview. **61%, up 9 points since April 2024; 2,500 workers in US, UK, Germany** (Greenhouse, 2024-12-10; medium)
  - Takeaway: Silence is common and not personal; follow up once or twice, then move on.
- The same survey reported some interviewers dismissed candidates for not sending a thank-you. **nearly one in five** (TopResume, n.d.; low)
- In a TopResume survey, most hiring managers and recruiters said thank-you notes matter. **68% said it matters; sample size and date not stated** (TopResume, n.d.; low)
  - Takeaway: A thank-you note is cheap insurance; send one.
