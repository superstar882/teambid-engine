# Remote jobs

Group: Search · Related vault file: `Job/Playbook/Where to Find Jobs.md` · 6 sources, 13 findings

## Findings (strongest sources first)

- Recruiters can filter for candidates who indicated they are open to remote work. (LinkedIn Help, n.d.; high)
  - Takeaway: Set Open to Work location types to include remote plus Bay Area onsite/hybrid.
- Tech is the most flexible industry. **94% of US tech firms offer work location flexibility** (Flex Index (Scoop / Work Forward), hosted by LSE, 2025; high)
  - Takeaway: Tech firms remain the best bet for remote/hybrid roles.
- Two-thirds of US firms (9,000+ tracked) still offer some location flexibility; one-third require full-time office. **66% flexible, 34% full-time in office (industry-adjusted)** (Flex Index (Scoop / Work Forward), hosted by LSE, 2025; high)
  - Takeaway: Hybrid is the common model; fully remote is a subset.
- LinkedIn data showed remote roles were a small share of postings but drew nearly half of applications. **Remote: 10% of open roles in Dec 2023, 46% of applications** (Fortune, 2024-01-29; high)
  - Takeaway: Remote postings have several times the competition; apply early and use referrals for them.
- Small companies are far more flexible than large ones. **67% of companies under 500 employees are Fully Flexible** (Flex Index (Scoop / Work Forward), hosted by LSE, 2025; high)
  - Takeaway: Target startups and small companies for remote roles; big tech mostly wants office days.
- Remote job postings declined while worker demand rose. **Remote postings down 9% from Jan 2022 to Dec 2023** (Fortune, 2024-01-29; high)
- Software development's remote share dropped over the year. **Software development and IT each down 3.8 percentage points year over year** (Indeed Hiring Lab, 2024-11-19; high)
  - Takeaway: Software remote roles are shrinking faster than average; expect hybrid offers.
- Remote/hybrid postings on Indeed fell from their 2022 peak but stayed about three times pre-pandemic levels. **7.8% (Oct 2024) vs 10.4% peak (Feb 2022) and 2.6% (Jan 2019)** (Indeed Hiring Lab, 2024-11-19; high)
  - Takeaway: Remote isn't gone, but it is under 10% of postings.
- Fortune 100 firms are tightening office requirements. **45% require 4-5 days in office; 29% full-time in office** (Flex Index (Scoop / Work Forward), hosted by LSE, 2025; high)
- Technology postings are only slightly more flexible. **Tech: 85% on-site, 11% hybrid, 4% remote** (Robert Half, 2026; medium)
  - Takeaway: Bay Area onsite/hybrid roles are the bigger pool.
- Entry-level roles are the least likely to be remote. **Entry-level (0-2 yrs): 8% hybrid, 2% remote vs senior 12% hybrid, 4% remote** (Robert Half, 2026; medium)
  - Takeaway: As early-career, remote is hardest to get; local presence is an advantage.
- Robert Half's analysis of US job postings found most new roles are fully on-site in Q2 2026. **87% on-site, 10% hybrid, 3% fully remote (Q2 2026); on-site was 65% in Q4 2025** (Robert Half, 2026; medium)
  - Takeaway: Fully remote postings are scarce; don't make remote-only the whole plan.
- Most surveyed job seekers prefer remote or hybrid work. **Remote 47.5%, hybrid 42.4%, onsite 10.1% preference** (Huntr, 2026; medium)
  - Takeaway: Being open to Bay Area onsite/hybrid is a competitive edge since most applicants want remote.
