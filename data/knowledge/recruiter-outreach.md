# Recruiters and outreach

Group: Search · Related vault file: `Job/Playbook/Where to Find Jobs.md` · 6 sources, 10 findings

## Findings (strongest sources first)

- Individually sent InMails beat bulk sends, and most replies come within a day. **Individual 15% higher response; 65% of replies within 24 hours, 90% within a week** (LinkedIn Talent Blog, 2022-05-19; high)
  - Takeaway: If no reply after a week, follow up once; don't wait longer.
- Personalized InMails and Recommended Matches get higher acceptance from candidates. **Personalized InMails: 40% higher acceptance; Recommended Matches: 35% more likely to accept** (LinkedIn Talent Blog, 2023-05-17; high)
- Short InMails get more replies; long ones get fewer. **Under 400 chars: 22% above average; over 1,200 chars: 11% below** (LinkedIn Talent Blog, 2022-05-19; high)
  - Takeaway: Keep LinkedIn messages to managers/recruiters under ~400 characters.
- Recruiter-sourced candidates have the highest conversion rate of any channel. **Recruiter-sourced: 2.9% of applications, 9.7% of hires (2025)** (Greenhouse, 2026-03; high)
  - Takeaway: Being findable by in-house recruiters (LinkedIn keywords, Open to Work) pays off.
- Larger companies saw higher reply rates from engineers to recruiting emails. **Engineering reply 18% at 1-249 FTE vs 29% at 5,000+ FTE** (Gem, n.d.; medium)
- Use the first sentence to show why talking to you adds value, not how you found them, and ask for a conversation rather than a job. (interviewing.io, n.d.; medium)
- Email hiring managers instead of LinkedIn, since managers live in email. (interviewing.io, n.d.; medium)
  - Takeaway: Find work emails for managers at target companies rather than relying on InMail.
- Cold outreach to recruiters and agency recruiters reaching out ranked among the least effective channels; cold outreach to hiring managers was net positive. (interviewing.io, n.d.; medium)
  - Takeaway: Skip cold-messaging recruiters; message the engineering manager instead.
- In nearly 2 million recruiting email sequences, engineers were the least likely group to reply to recruiter outreach. **Engineers 24% reply vs Product 43%; average open rate 83% for 3-stage sequences** (Gem, n.d.; medium)
- Short, personalized emails to hiring managers that show common ground can get much higher reply rates than generic accomplishment-led notes. **25-50% expected for common-ground emails; 5-25% for accomplishment-led** (interviewing.io, n.d.; low)
