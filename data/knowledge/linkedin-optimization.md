# LinkedIn optimization

Group: Search · Related vault file: `Job/Profile/Candidate Profile.md` · 8 sources, 10 findings

## Findings (strongest sources first)

- Recruiters get notified when a candidate in their pipeline switches to Open to Work. (LinkedIn Help, n.d.; high)
- Members who share Open to Work with recruiters only still appear in the Recruiter 'Open to work' spotlight, just without the photo frame. (LinkedIn Help, n.d.; high)
  - Takeaway: Recruiters-only Open to Work gets the search benefit privately.
- Adding more weak ties led users to apply to more jobs and land more jobs. (MIT Sloan School of Management (press release on Rajkumar et al., Science 2022), 2022-09-15; high)
  - Takeaway: Keep growing a relevant LinkedIn network (people in AI/data eng at target companies).
- Candidates marked Open to Work respond to recruiter InMails at a higher rate than others. **37% higher response rate** (LinkedIn Talent Blog, 2022-05-19; high)
  - Takeaway: Turning on Open to Work (recruiters-only) makes recruiters more likely to reach out.
- LinkedIn says members with the public Open to Work frame are more likely to get recruiter InMails. **40% more likely to receive InMails; 200M have the feature on privately** (Fortune, 2024-09-05; high)
  - Takeaway: Open to Work increases recruiter contact.
- Nearly half of LinkedIn hirers explicitly use skills data when filling roles. **48%** (LinkedIn Talent Blog, 2024-04-04; high)
  - Takeaway: Skills listed on the profile directly feed recruiter search and AI matching.
- LinkedIn Recruiter's AI drafts personalized InMails from the candidate's profile details, the job description and company info. (LinkedIn Talent Blog, 2023-05-17; high)
  - Takeaway: A detailed profile (projects, results) gives recruiter AI more to match and mention.
- Members who list at least one skill get more profile views and messages. **Up to 2x profile views and connection requests, up to 4x messages** (LinkedIn Talent Blog, 2024-04-04; high)
  - Takeaway: Fill the skills section fully (up to 50) with exact terms: RAG, LangChain, PySpark, Kafka, FastAPI.
- LinkedIn allows up to 50 skills and lets members feature up to 5 in the About section. **50 skills; 5 in About** (LinkedIn Talent Blog, 2024-04-04; high)
  - Takeaway: Pin the 5 skills that match target roles (RAG/LLM apps, Python, data engineering).
- Some recruiters and career coaches say the public green frame can look desperate or invite lowball offers. (Fortune, 2024-09-05; medium)
  - Takeaway: Use the recruiters-only setting to get found without the public frame.
